The Woman Behind The Mask

Anjisha
Pun

A talented bassist and fierce protector, transformed by fate into Earth-65's legendary Ghost-Spider.

In an alternate universe where Anjisha Pun was bitten by the radioactive spider instead of Peter Parker, she became the spectacular Spider-Woman. After the tragic loss of her best friend Peter, she dedicated herself to being the hero her world needed.

By day, the bassist for the band "The Mary Janes." By night, the vigilant protector who leaps across dimensions and swings through the Spider-Verse. Her ballet training gives her unmatched grace, while her fierce determination makes her unstoppable.
